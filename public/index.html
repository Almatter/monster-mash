A BRIEF GUIDE TO DEVASTATION

How to reign

Move with WASD / arrows or the left thumb stick. Your claws attack automatically. Aim with your mouse; on touch, aim follows movement and nearby prey.

1 / Q · Rupture — shockwave and collision kills.

2 / E · Devour — eat nearby prey to heal and enrage.

3 / R · Death beam — sweep your aim during its burst.

4 / Space · Catastrophe — annihilate the surrounding horde.

Keep killing to raise Carnage up to ×5. Kills award 10–1,500 base Dominance, multiplied by Carnage. Waves, multikills, and feats award explicit bonuses. Your power grows each wave.

Stay out of red titan rings. Use Devour often. Your next incarnation starts fresh.
