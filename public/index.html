How to reign

Move with WASD / arrows or the left thumb stick. Basic attacks fire automatically when prey is in range. Aim with your mouse; touch aim follows movement, then locks to nearby prey when stationary.

Use Q / E / R / Space or 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 for your selected monster's powers. Calamity places Starfall and Vortex with a second tap or click; Cancel or Escape clears the marker. Reaper suggests a target for Blink and Arc: press the same power again to confirm, or click/tap to choose. Devourer gains rapid Lunge charges through Release. Lunge follows movement, or your last movement direction when stationary, and each cast briefly dodges damage. Check the kit in the registry. P / Escape pauses.

Kills add Carnage up to ×5. It decays gradually after 1.6 seconds without a kill. Enemy base points × Carnage earn Dominance. Waves, multikills and Run Feats award additional bonuses.

Stage 2: hunt all 10 captains, claim their seals and choose a boon after each victory. Flank guards or break their protection. Healing oases help you recover. Faster clears earn more Dominance.

In Stage 1, survive to automatically reach Unbound I at 2:30, Unbound II at 5:00, Unbound III at 7:00 and Final Release at 8:30. Each release strengthens your kit. There is no win timer; aim for a longer, higher-scoring run. You can end an incarnation from the pause menu.

Run Feats repeat within a run and award score. Monster Records and prestige Titles persist locally. Inspect all requirements in Monster Records. Prestige titles are cosmetic; Earn The Gatebreaker with one champion to enter Stage 2 from October 8. Monster Records shows both ways to qualify. Stay out of red hostile rings.

TAKE YOUR PROGRESS WITH YOU

Finish your run, export a backup, then import it on your other device before playing there. Your titles, Gatebreaker credit, mastery, records, name and colors travel with you.

How progress is combined

Earned rewards and each champion’s qualifying days are combined. Bests and lifetime counters keep the higher value, so importing twice never adds the same kills or runs twice. Always transfer your latest backup before switching devices: unsynced lifetime totals from separate play on both devices cannot be added together. This is a manual backup, not automatic sync. Sound and FX settings stay on each device. Stage opening dates still apply.
