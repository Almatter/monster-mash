Battlefield rules

Watch the battle or take control of any claimant. Everyone fights by the same prototype rules. Every wave follows its home lane to the central road, then enters the target base along that base’s own exit lane. Bases are spaced farther apart. Armies reserve different neighboring bases using distance from home, focus one guardian at a time, then attack the crystal. After the first base falls, they prefer the neighbor on the other side of home. Orders rebalance only when needed to keep targets distinct. Minions only stop for enemies immediately adjacent to them, then continue their assigned route without chasing. Inspect the army orders below to see each target. Bring a minion wave to absorb heavy guardian fire. The dashed outer ring shows guardian range; the inner circle shows the crystal’s firing area. Both guardians defend only this local perimeter, never shoot neighboring guardians/crystals or enemies inside their own home base, and prioritize in-range attackers of their champion. They strongly resist unsupported champion attacks. Guardians block champion hits from outside their response ring. Bring at least three minions into that ring to open a siege window. Easy, Normal and Hard gradually build siege pressure: supported structure damage increases, the required escort drops to a minimum of one, and crystal-less rivals lose sustain. Solo attacks still face guardian armor and heavy retaliation. Hell has no timed escalation. The crystal fires hard inside its own circle and blocks champion hits originating outside it. Defeat both guardians to remove the crystal’s ward. Your champion, minions and surviving base share local vision. The overview respects fog while you control a champion; watch mode remains omniscient. Rivals in Inspect show only their last scouted stats and build, with the age of the report. Enemy minions assigned to a recalling champion’s base gain 35% speed and 60% damage until that champion returns. Sell stat ranks or augments beside your living crystal or during recall for 80% of their purchase price. Healing cannot be resold. Ordinary spell hits are limited to 800 world units and shared vision to prevent distant cross-lane farming; planted mines and existing poison continue locally. Full map (M) opens a live overview without pausing; close it to return to your champion. Select a champion to follow in watch mode below. A surviving crystal recalls its champion after 10 seconds, plus 4 seconds per repeat death and 3 seconds per three match minutes (maximum 60 seconds); without it, death is final. Return with all abilities ready. Earn mana over time and through your own combat contributions; spend it beside your living crystal or during recall on stat upgrades and ability augments. Healing is purchased only while alive. AI champions keep their native kits and choose varied seeded builds: at most sixteen stat ranks and two ability augments. Rival vision matches the close camera, with delayed reactions, aim updates and one focus at a time. The vision / focus overlay below makes their attention visible. Outfarm rivals to fund repeated army training and purchased siege beasts in the Army shop. Siege beasts never spawn on a timer: each costs 1,000 mana, with one active per army and a 90-second purchase cooldown. Training has no rank cap and escalating costs. Brood muster increases reinforcement rate, with six minions per wave down to a one-second interval and an unchanged living troop limit. Shopping is also available during recall while your crystal survives. It closes if your crystal falls or you leave it after returning. Crystal healing now costs mana.

New battle shuffles the seed. Replay / use seed repeats the seed entered here. Changing it changes starting neighbors and AI choices. Troop limits apply equally to all armies.

    PC: WASD / arrows move; Q / E / R or 1 / 2 / 3 use abilities; Space or 4 uses the ultimate. Aim: Manual lets you choose a point on the ground; press the ability twice for automatic placement. Pause can then stop the battle separately. Watch mode lets all eight act autonomously.
