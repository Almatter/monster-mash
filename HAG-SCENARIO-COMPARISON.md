# Hag scenario screening — October 16, 2026

Measured build: `7813e3d`. No gameplay balance values were changed.

**These are automated pilot outcomes, not human player averages or calibrated human win rates.**

Five fixed-seed normal-health active-bot trials per champion per stage; 30 Hz simulation, 0.2-second decisions. Seeds 77,123,444,835,1217. Stage 1 capped at 900 seconds; others at 1200. Means include all outcomes. Clears and timeouts censor survival, so elapsed time is run duration, not expected lifetime. Separate three-seed Hag/Reaper checks at 60 Hz. No balance edits, artificial healing, health overrides or forced kills.

The pilot kites at ranged distance or closes for melee, uses legal movement and cooldowns, aims supported abilities, lets Hag gas tick until its final second before ignition, and uses flight when threatened at low health. Hunt pilots seek captains/seals and healing oases and choose legal boons. Realm pilots read actual randomized posts, know the solution, seek required shrines, heal at cleared altars, equip the matching four relics and avoid some boss warnings. War pilots initially defend near a friendly sentry, choose perks and then seek enemies; the player retains their native kit while NPC champions can inherit powers normally.

## Interpretation

- **Stage 1:** Hag is last in both observed survival and Dominance in this sample.
- **Stage 2:** Hag clears 0/5; every other champion clears 5/5. Her longer elapsed time represents failed hunts, not superior survival performance.
- **Stage 3:** Judge scores and completion together: successful runs replace native powers with the solution relics. This does not isolate native-kit boss DPS.
- **Stage 4:** Very early deaths affect several champions, not just Hag. Starting camps are champion-specific, NPC kits differ, and native-only player builds do not represent optimized captured-power builds. Treat this as evidence to investigate overall war balance and spawn pressure, not a precise PvP ranking.
- Five seeds are a screening sample. Policies are not equally skilled with every kit, warning avoidance is incomplete, and any navigation stall can reduce a score. No confidence intervals or population win-rate claims are justified here.

## Reproduction and evidence

- `node tools/champion-scenarios.mjs 1` (replace 1 with stage 2, 3 or 4). Optional seeds and champion IDs are comma-separated positional arguments.
- Set `SCENARIO_HZ=60`, `SCENARIO_LABEL=60hz` for the sensitivity set; use seeds `77,123,444` and champions `hag,reaper`.
- Raw five-seed rows, summaries and the sensitivity runs are retained in `test-results/champion-scenarios-comparison.json` (ignored generated output).
- Stage 3 was processed in separate OS processes by roster groups to reduce runtime; duplicate pilot rows were replaced by matching final-policy rows keyed by champion and seed.
- The 15 existing Stage 4/Hag integration tests passed. This confirms functional behavior, not balance parity.
