# Devourer effect sprite prompts

Both source masters were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized to transparent 768×768 WebP by `node tools/pack-devourer-vfx.mjs`. They are original effect sprites; the old `devourer.webp` remains for other ability impacts.

## `devourer-claw.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D top-down game VFX sprite for Monster Mash, Devourer's rapidly repeating basic attack. Create an anime isekai dark-fantasy hand-painted effect, matching a pale bone-clawed predator and crimson-black magic. Three asymmetrical curved claw slashes and torn crimson energy wisps occupy the OUTER annulus of a square image, about 55%-95% radius. Very clear fully transparent central circle occupying roughly 50% of image diameter so the playable monster remains visible beneath it. Strong directional brushwork, jagged organic edges, inked line accents, pale ivory cutting edges with restrained dark blood-red sparks, visual energy that implies a fast slash around the champion's attack perimeter. Designed to be rotated/scaled and faded for 0.22-second animation in a dark stone arena; crisp readable silhouette at small size. Actual transparent background/alpha, no floor, no checkerboard, no UI, no words, no character, no complete solid magic circle, no stock vector arcs, no watermark.

## `devourer-unbound-aura.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D top-down game VFX sprite for Monster Mash, Devourer's persistent Unbound evolution aura. Create a dark anime isekai hand-painted supernatural predator aura, inspired by bone-white claws and crimson-black void energy. A loose asymmetrical circular arrangement of wispy ember-red smoke, a few thin ivory bone shards, and torn manga-ink energy ribbons, especially around the OUTER perimeter. Make this visually different from an attack slash: subtle, persistent, mystical and ominous, no large weapon blades. The center 55% of the square must be fully transparent so the playable monster remains visible. Outer edges fade to transparent, no opaque backdrop. It should look like an illustrated magical presence on dark medieval stone, not a stock vector magic circle, no smooth geometric ellipses, no runes or text, no character, no floor, no watermark. Actual transparent alpha background; square game sprite suitable for gentle rotation/pulsation and stage-based scaling.
