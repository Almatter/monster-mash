# Character material refinement

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool with the existing master as the sole reference. Guides supply region labels only; the shipped bases retain the original drawing and source shading. Rebuild with `node tools/refine-character-materials.mjs` and `node tools/process-reaper-art.mjs`.

Guide: `art-source/sovereign/selection-color-guide.png`

Source: `art-source/sovereign/selection-master.png`

Prompt:

Edit this EXACT existing character art into a technical material segmentation guide for recoloring. Preserve every silhouette, pose, placement, proportions, facial structure, outline and tiny ornament exactly aligned with the source. Do NOT redraw, redesign, add elements, move or crop the character. Replace materials with continuous solid flat label colors: BLUE #0000FF for blue armor plates; MAGENTA #FF00FF for all purple hair, horns, cape and fabric; YELLOW #FFFF00 for all gold metal trim/chains; RED #FF0000 for all cyan energy, jewels and eyes; CYAN #00FFFF for the exposed face, ears and skin. Do not use shading, texture, speckling, gradients or lighting in the label fills. Preserve only the thin black ink outlines; fill shadows and highlights with their surrounding material's same solid label color. Remove the entire background to transparency, including any green background. The image is a mask guide, not a new illustration. Same canvas aspect ratio and full extent as reference.
