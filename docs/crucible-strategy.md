# Hard and Hell strategy (v53)

Combat damage, reaction delays, aiming error, ordinary troop limits and the humanized field of view retain their existing difficulty settings. Strategy adds decisions rather than damage or resource cheats. Easy and Normal retain their previous strategic behavior.

## Hard: supported recall pushes

When the owner of its assigned siege target dies, a rival evaluates its own health, available wave, champion/wave travel and the remaining public recall timer. Hard requires a nearby supported opportunity and leaves a three-second margin before the defender returns; Hell considers a wider area and a smaller margin. A committed champion clears enemies blocking its wave, waits outside base retaliation range until support opens the siege, then focuses a visible guardian or crystal. Routine shopping and unrelated farming no longer override the commitment. Loss of support, low health, a threat at home, a nearby third-party champion, the deadline or the defender returning ends the push and prompts disengagement. Existing Hell-only crystal-finishing trades remain available independently.

Champions in a lane do not gain remote army-command access. They exploit an existing assigned wave. Attention still respects local vision, notice delays, sampled aim, explicit invulnerability and native kit/mana rules.

## Hell: adaptive purchases

Every eight seconds, a rival can update two counter priorities from scouting reports no older than 45 seconds. It prefers its current opponent, otherwise a recent known rival. Examples include Withering Strikes against healing/ward builds, Might against stacked health/armor, armor and health against heavy offense, Antivenom against Hag, and control resistance against observed duration-focused Titan/Lycanthrope builds. Army investments can also inform a pressure-oriented Haste preference. Unseen purchases do not change the plan until scouted.

The original native kit and randomized augment/build identity remain. Counter purchases compete for the same sixteen stat ranks and use normal prices. A full build can replace one non-priority rank at the living crystal or during recall, paying the existing 20% resale loss; replacements are at least twelve seconds apart. No purchase, refund, extra slot or free currency is granted in the field.

## Hell: conditional siege preparation

A visible siege making less than roughly 8% progress over twenty seconds, or repeated depletion of its own army later in the match, can justify a beast after some champion development. This is conditional, not a periodic spawn. A near-funded rival can save mana while continuing to farm instead of returning home to wait for passive income. At home it pays the ordinary siege price and cooldown. Only after a beast exists does it rally troops, gathering a small wave for up to eight seconds, then issue a legal release order and escort the push. A beast already on the road is escorted without recalling its existing wave.

Champions remain outside the base's response ring until sufficient troops or an existing breach permit supported attacks. They respond to visible champions threatening the escort, recover when badly hurt, and stop gathering if the beast is destroyed. Home danger interrupts preparation. Existing troop caps, spawn limits, crystal wards and unsupported-attack resistance continue to apply.

Tests include native-kit objective pressure versus an otherwise identical farming distraction, scouting privacy and expiry, full-build refunds and purchase limits, saving without idle camping, actual siege purchases, gathering/release, supported movement and interruption cases. Difficulty is not yet calibrated to a measured human win rate.
