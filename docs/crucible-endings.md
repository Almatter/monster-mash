# The last claim

Stage 4 changes from the war mix to the climactic Final mix when two claimants remain. It adds no announcement, map reveal or combat rule. A result switches to the quieter results score; a defeated player instead hears their own champion's theme in the new drumless defeat mix, plus a short lament in that character's tonal center. Existing mute and interface/music volumes apply.

Every champion has a distinct acceptance of the mantle, speech and description of their fall. The player's selected name and title introduce the ending. Victory establishes them as the undisputed Lord of the Horde, leading the winter invasion of the Second Continent. An early elimination does not invent a winner; the paragraph names the victor if the player stays until the match resolves. “Will you rise again when it calls?” leads into the Rise again button.

Hell victories alone play a brief ascension using the existing generated idle, attack and ultimate artwork. It uses the full-resolution, exactly recolored atlas and power effect already loaded for the player, then dissolves into their 512px portrait. It is a live canvas presentation, not a newly drawn animation or a downloaded GIF. It replays on request and stays still with reduced motion. Score-card image downloads remain static.

Champion deaths keep a rolling eight-second damage history, grouped into quarter-second source/type buckets. Records contain actual health and ward losses after mitigation, the final damage source and effects at death. The history has at most 160 groups per champion and 64 completed death reports. Reports are expandable in the existing battle events and the result card; no extra combat popup is introduced. They describe past damage and do not disclose a rival's unscouted build. Healing within the window may make cumulative losses exceed maximum health.
